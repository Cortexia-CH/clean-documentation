Cigarette

Cigarette or cigarette butts. Can be flattened. Can be white and orange or completely white.
Don't mistake with Iqos sticks!

Real life example


Iqos sticks

Iqos sticks look like cigarettes but a few features makes us able to differenciate them:

  • Fully white
  • Do not burn, just heat (=> always longer than cigarette butts)
  • Thiner than a regular cigarette
  • Never flat: they keep a nice shape because they are more solid than cigarettes
