Drain/sewer grate

Clear drain/sewer grate

Metal grids or bars covering rainwater drains. Rectangular or curved, often embedded in streets or sidewalks.


Do not tag manhole covers.

Obstructed drain/sewer grate

Drain (partially) obstructed by other objects (leaves, paper, etc.), which might compromise the water flow.
The obstruction must be significant.
Please tag the obstructing objects separately.
